About me

Designing learning that respects people's time.

I’m V’Laun Banks, a Senior Instructional Designer with more than eight years of experience creating outcomes-focused learning for healthcare and corporate audiences.

My work spans curriculum development, interactive eLearning, facilitator-led training, onboarding, performance support, LMS implementation, and Train-the-Trainer programs. I partner closely with subject matter experts and stakeholders to turn complex content into clear learning experiences.

I’m especially interested in solutions that help learners perform confidently on the job—not simply complete a course.
